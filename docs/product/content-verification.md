# Human Content Verification Backlog

## Principle

**UNKNOWN CONTENT IS NOT AN UNKNOWN REQUIREMENT.**

The product can be designed and implemented to hold verified content even when a name, date, total, policy, biography, schedule, or claim is not yet confirmed. Unknown facts belong here; they must not be invented, copied from Wix as if current, or treated as architectural blockers.

Some items are publication or launch gates for the affected page or claim. That is different from blocking application implementation. If evidence is unavailable, the safe outcome is to omit, qualify, archive, or clearly date the content—not to publish an unsupported assertion.

## Backlog workflow

### States

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Unverified | Candidate fact or claim has not been checked against an accountable source. |
| In review | A named reviewer is comparing sources or seeking approval. |
| Verified | Evidence, approving owner, scope, and effective/review date are recorded. |
| Approved with qualification | Publishable only with recorded date, scope, caveat, or attribution. |
| Retire / do not publish | No continuing value, insufficient evidence, revoked permission, or unacceptable risk. |
| Scheduled for review | Verified content has a future owner and review date. |

### Evidence record required for verification

Each verified item should record:

- exact approved wording or structured value;
- authoritative source or artifact location;
- approving person or role;
- effective date, reporting period, or as-of date;
- next review date or event that triggers review;
- pages/records affected;
- privacy, consent, licensing, or legal conditions; and
- disposition of conflicting legacy versions.

An unowned email, copied Wix paragraph, search snippet, or unlabeled photograph is discovery evidence—not final approval.

### Priority

- **P0 — affected experience cannot launch safely:** identity, location/contact, eligibility/intake, transaction, privacy/consent, legal, or material operational facts.
- **P1 — required for launch quality/trust:** leadership, core Programs, current ReStore, current giving/volunteer/event journeys, foundational impact evidence.
- **P2 — may launch later or in reduced form:** historical depth, non-current campaigns/events, optional biographies, archives, extended partner/grant recognition.

## Cross-record verification rules

### Conflicts

When sources disagree, record both sources privately, choose an accountable approver, and publish only the approved value with an as-of date where appropriate. Do not preserve multiple contradictory public statements “for completeness.”

### Dates and freshness

Operational content should use one of these controls:

- effective start/end dates;
- publication and updated dates;
- reviewed-at and next-review dates;
- event edition dates and status; or
- an explicit historical label.

The right control depends on the content type. A generic “last updated” timestamp is not proof that a fact was reviewed.

### Privacy and consent

Consent must be specific enough to support the intended medium, channel, duration, audience, identity detail, and reuse. Public Story or Project relations must never be used to infer applicant status, eligibility, services received, address, family composition, or sensitive circumstances beyond what was deliberately approved.

### Metrics and progress

Campaign progress and impact measures require defined numerator, denominator/goal, source system, update method, as-of time, adjustment/refund treatment where relevant, and approver. A marketing estimate must be labeled as such or omitted.

### External links and embeds

Verification includes more than “the URL opens.” Test destination ownership, purpose, mobile behavior, keyboard/accessibility, privacy/consent copy, success/failure/cancel behavior, and a named maintainer. Retest transaction and intake destinations before launch and on a scheduled cadence.

## Review cadence and completion reporting

At content-readiness reviews, report:

- P0/P1/P2 counts by state;
- items without an accountable owner;
- items whose evidence or approval expired;
- affected routes/records held from publication;
- conflicting legacy content disposition;
- participant/media permissions at risk; and
- external destinations that failed retesting.

The backlog is complete enough for a given launch slice when every fact exposed by that slice is Verified, Approved with qualification, or intentionally Retire / do not publish. A lower-priority unknown outside the slice remains a content task, not a reason to halt unrelated implementation.
